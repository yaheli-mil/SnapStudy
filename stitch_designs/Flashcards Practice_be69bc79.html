QUESTION • 3 PTS

What is the primary function of the mitochondria in eukaryotic cells, and which metabolic cycle takes place within its inner membrane matrix?

help_outline

Hint: Often termed the "powerhouse", it handles aerobic respiration involving acetyl-CoA.

CONFIRMED ANSWER

Mitochondria generate cellular energy via ATP synthesis. The Krebs Cycle (Citric Acid Cycle) occurs inside the inner mitochondrial matrix.
